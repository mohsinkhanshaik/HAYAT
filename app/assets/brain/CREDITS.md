HAYAT adaptations: scene transforms applied, centred, uniformly scaled,
Y-axis reflected with triangle winding preserved, textures omitted, three
simplified detail levels, and interface theme colours. No endorsement by the
creator is implied. The model is a visual aid, not measured neural activity.

`manifest.json` records the pinned dataset revision, original GLB checksum,
derived geometry checksum and triangle counts. `scripts/prepare_brain_asset.py`
reproduces this geometry from the matching original GLB. The application loads
the bundled JSON offline; it does not contact Sketchfab or Hugging Face.
