# Getting started with HAYAT

Related research windows now share **Other research tools** for manual navigation.
An image handoff prepares its path without reading or searching it. In Professional
research, save/export/history and owner-presence commands are under **Report actions**.
See the window-by-window GUI audit.

## Image files and source evidence

Open **Studios → Local image tools** or ask **Check local image toolkit**.
Choose one local file to inspect its technical metadata, or two files to compare
bytes, normalized pixels and perceptual hash distances. No analysis starts when
the window opens. The compact window keeps its report scrollable.

For publication evidence, ask `Find image copies on the web for "D:/photos/source.png"`,
or include a public source-page URL. The file stays local. Hash discovery covers
Wikimedia Commons only; **Searches → Image research → Check source page** compares
images actually retrieved from a supplied public page. No results mean unknown
coverage, not absence from the internet. This does not identify people from faces.

Optional ImageHash, ExifRead and PyWavelets are installed inside the project
environment. Manual setup: `.venv\Scripts\python.exe scripts/setup_image_tools.py --install`.
Run the same command with `--check` for a read-only inventory.

## Private networking

**Find people to contact** (2026-10-25) finds who to reach when you don't have a
name yet. Open **Studios → Private networking → Find people to contact**, or ask
**Find people to contact: founders of fintech startups in India**. It reads
public professional sources: leaders of organisations (Wikidata), researchers
(OpenAlex), developers (GitHub) and organisations' own team pages. Each person
comes with the source that names them and ways in (the organisation's website, a
published profile, a LinkedIn search you open). Save the ones you want to the
contact book; every search is kept under **Earlier searches**. See
[finding people to contact](people-finder.md).

**Find professional contacts** now has a **deep** setting (five queries, up to
12 public pages), an explicit required-Tor public-web route, and visible source
coverage. **Organization onion sources** prepares a separate organization-only
query without starting a search. See upstream references and checks.

Open **Studios → Private networking**, or ask **Show my private networking contacts**.
Save people you know and public professional sources, record how a contact route
was obtained, compare entered expertise with your goal and prepare an unsent
introduction. Matches explain topic overlap; availability and willingness to
help remain unknown. The local book uses Windows account encryption. Use its
native form for sensitive details: ordinary chat history and explicit plaintext
exports are outside that encrypted book.

Use **Find professional contacts** to search by a known name and organization.
Individual candidates and published team routes stay separate, with source
hashes/timestamps and explicit saving. Try **Find professional contacts for Ada
Example at Example Institute** in chat. No outreach is sent.

In **Searches**, choose **Tor web (public internet)** for a general topic such
as AI through Tor. **Onion (Tor)** remains onion-only discovery. Blocked indexes
are reported without bypassing restrictions. People, Tor and REA update.

Open **Studios → Operation view** for a draggable desktop sample and recorded
tool events. Automatic Windows previews are click-through and do not take
keyboard focus. Open the view manually to move it or pause observation; use
the main **Emergency stop** to stop work. The viewer samples only while an
operation is active or when you press **Observe once**.

Open **Studios → Onion page navigator** to read a known public v3 onion URL
through installed Tor. Same-site following is bounded and optional. Inspect
retrieval evidence and links; cancellation, privacy and normal permissions
still apply. See [navigation](onion-navigation.md) and the
GUI upgrade record.

## Replica app studio

Open **Studios → Replica app studio**, or ask `Replica workflows`. Prepare a

## Reverse engineer a binary or app

Open **Studios → Reverse engineering (REA)** to browse local analysis operations,
choose a file or folder, and inspect results without blocking the chat. Try
**Reverse engineer C:\apps\thing.exe** in chat. Static JavaScript analysis works
with Node.js; native pseudocode needs a compatible Ghidra, Hopper or IDA engine.
See [the REA guide](reverse-engineering.md) for setup and verification limits.

Explore twelve fields, search for a topic, check prerequisites, and prepare a lesson,

HAYAT is an assistant that runs on your own computer. You talk to it in your own words, by typing or by voice, and it does the work: it answers, looks things up, makes files and pictures, uses apps, and keeps track of your money.

## Your first five minutes

Press **All features** (**Ctrl+Shift+A**) to search every feature, workspace and
tool. Press **Activity** (**Ctrl+Shift+L**) to see recorded actions, background
work and failures. The main window keeps sensor and monitoring states visible.
See [navigation and transparency](gui-discovery-and-transparency.md).

1. Type what you want in the box at the bottom, in your own words, and press Send. Or press Start voice and speak.
2. HAYAT does the work and shows you the result. It only sends, installs or changes things when you ask, and checks with you before risky steps.
3. Not sure? Ask "can you ...?" about anything, or pick an area below and press Try.

Press **Help** at the top of the window, or press **F1**, at any time. It opens **What can HAYAT do?**: every area below, whether it is ready, how to start, and examples with a **Try** button that puts the example in the chat box for you. Hover over any button to see what it does.

You can also just ask HAYAT:

```text
What can you do?
Help me start a business
Can you make invoices?
I'm new here
```

## How HAYAT keeps you safe

- HAYAT only sends messages, installs software or changes things when you ask. Installing, deleting and other risky steps ask you to confirm.
- Publishing your website, registering an account or a business, and submitting any web form always follow the same steps. HAYAT prepares it, shows you every value and a screenshot, and waits. You type any password yourself and confirm. HAYAT does it once, checks the result, and you say whether it is right. It never pays or buys anything, and never solves CAPTCHAs.
- The camera and microphone only turn on when you ask, and never in privacy mode.
- Passwords stay in the Windows credential store. HAYAT refuses to keep full card or account numbers.
- Everything runs on this computer. There are no paid services behind it.
